<h2 id="donnees">Personal data</h2>

The information sent with the booking request form (name, email, telephone, dates, group size) is used only to reply to your request. It is passed by the Web3Forms service to the campsite's mailbox, kept for 12 months at most and never sold. You can ask to see, correct or delete it by writing to info@campingclairac.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr).
